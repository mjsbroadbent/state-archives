Copies and archival certificates

Copy Section · room 012

We make photocopies, microfilm prints and photographs of documents, issue archival extracts, and certify work records and wages for pensions from the records of the collective and state farms.

Requests by post. A request is registered when the applicant's stamped addressed envelope reaches the Copy Section. Requests by e-mail are not accepted; requests by telephone are not accepted either.
