Records under classification review

Closed shelf

Records of the Soviet and territorial periods are generally open. The records listed below are subject to classification review. They are not issued in the reading room, copies are not made, and their inventories are not published.

    Questions

    Why can I not see the files on the 2007 privatizations?
    They are subject to classification review.
    When will the review be completed?
    When the review is complete, the records will be listed in the guide to the fonds and issued in the reading room.
    Who carries out the review?
    The review is carried out in accordance with the procedure in force.
    May I see the inventory of a fond under review?
    The inventories of records under review are subject to classification review.
    Researchers have cited documents from Fond 310.
    Selected documents of inventory 1 of Fond 310 are open and may be ordered in the reading room.
    I have asked before.
    Your request is in the register. Requests are answered in the order received when the review is complete.

    Request access

    Requests for access to records under review are entered in the register of requests.
